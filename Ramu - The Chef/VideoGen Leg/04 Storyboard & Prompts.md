# 04 Storyboard & Prompts — Twelve Glasses

Written 21:18 IST. Portrait 9:16, Seedance. Twelve shots, ~80 s. Paste each prompt as the shot's prompt text; attach the named references (character, location, prop) in the shot's References block — a name in the prompt is *not* an attached reference (tool's own warning).

Conventions in every prompt: portrait frame · Ramu named exactly as in the cast ("Ramu Prasad") · age stated · "same faded red bandana" · one light source named · explicit avoids at the end. Narration is Ramu's voice (Manav), past tense, few words. If the tool has an on-screen text / title-card field, use the "Card" text; if not, skip cards — narration carries the meaning.

Cut order if behind: 9, then 6, then 2. Never 7, 8, 12.

## Shot 1 · 6 s · Naturalist · refs: Ramu Prasad, Sunrise Chai Stall, Steel Tea Kettle and Glasses

```
Extreme close-up, portrait frame. Ramu Prasad aged fifteen, no moustache, same faded red bandana, pours tea from a blackened steel kettle held high into a small ribbed glass; steam rises through the first orange light of dawn. Rack focus from the pouring stream up to his young face, eyes fixed on the glass. Sunrise Chai Stall behind him, blurred trucks on the road. Handheld, shallow depth of field, warm low sun, long shadows. Quiet, intent. No dialogue. Avoid: extra people, on-screen text, logos, cartoon look, cool blue grading.
```
Narration: **"I started with a glass."**
Risk: age — the reference is 25; if he renders adult, add "boyish, smooth-faced, slight" and retake once.

## Shot 2 · 6 s · Symmetry · refs: Ramu Prasad, Sunrise Chai Stall

```
Flat-on, dead-centre symmetrical wide shot, portrait frame, locked-off static camera. The whole Sunrise Chai Stall at dawn: kettle steaming on the burner, six glasses in a row on the counter, the boy Ramu Prasad aged fifteen standing centred behind the counter, perfectly still, looking straight into the lens. Empty road behind. Warm sun, long shadows, held for the full shot. Avoid: camera movement, extra people, on-screen text, cool blue grading.
```
Card: **"Back to work. Day 1."**
Risk: Seedance may add movement; "locked-off static" is repeated for that reason.

## Shot 3 · 7 s · Naturalist · refs: Ramu Prasad, Rashid's Highway Dhaba

```
Handheld medium close-up, portrait frame. Ramu Prasad aged twenty, thin moustache, same faded red bandana, at the mouth of a clay tandoor at Rashid's Highway Dhaba at night, face lit orange from below, slapping a naan onto the tandoor wall, sweat on his temples, smoke. Truck headlights sweep across the dark background. Cut from his hands to his face. Warm tandoor light, deep black shadows. Urgent, tireless. Avoid: extra people, on-screen text, daylight, fill light.
```

## Shot 4 · 6 s · Shadow · refs: Ramu Prasad, Rashid's Highway Dhaba

```
Silhouette, portrait frame. Ramu Prasad aged twenty in profile against the glowing orange mouth of the tandoor, smoke drifting, one hard warm light source, everything else black. Very slow push-in on his eyes as he glances at a chalkboard of orders and keeps working without stopping. Stark contrast, sweat visible on his skin. Avoid: extra people, on-screen text, fill light, cool blue grading.
```
Narration: **"Then a wall of fire."**

## Shot 5 · 7 s · Naturalist · refs: Ramu Prasad, City Trattoria Kitchen

```
Over-the-shoulder handheld tracking shot, portrait frame, following Ramu Prasad aged twenty-five, same faded red bandana, into the City Trattoria Kitchen: bright, loud, steam, harsh fluorescent tubes over warm heat lamps. He takes the junior station and copies a pasta toss in a pan; it slops over the edge; he resets and tries again, jaw set. Shallow depth of field, naturalistic. Other cooks only as blurred shapes. Avoid: other cooks' faces in focus, named extra characters, on-screen text.
```
Narration: **"Then a kitchen where nobody knew my name."**

## Shot 6 · 6 s · Naturalist · refs: Ramu Prasad, City Trattoria Kitchen

```
Extreme close-up, portrait frame. Ramu Prasad's scarred, burn-marked hands finishing a plate of pasta with precise tweezers and a wipe of the rim, City Trattoria Kitchen, warm heat-lamp light. Rack focus up to his face: no smile, eyes already moving to the next ticket on the rail. Shallow depth of field. Avoid: other people in focus, on-screen text.
```

## Shot 7 · 7 s · Shadow · refs: Ramu Prasad, Shuttered Trattoria Step, Knife Roll

```
Wide shot, portrait frame, static camera with a very slow push-in over the whole shot. Shuttered Trattoria Step at night in rain, metal grille down, CLOSED sign, one sodium street lamp. Ramu Prasad aged twenty-five sits on the step, the worn canvas Knife Roll on his knees, rain on his bandana and shoulders. The push-in ends on his face: jaw working, eyes down, no tears. Deep black shadows, single amber light, rain texture. Avoid: extra people, cars, on-screen text, slow motion.
```
Narration: **"Then nothing."**

## Shot 8 · 5 s · Shadow → Naturalist · refs: Ramu Prasad, Shuttered Trattoria Step, Knife Roll

```
Same wide framing, portrait frame, static camera. Shuttered Trattoria Step at grey dawn, rain stopped, wet pavement. Ramu Prasad aged twenty-five stands, shoulders the Knife Roll, and walks out of frame to the right. The camera does not follow; it holds on the empty step for two seconds. Cold grey light, quiet. Avoid: extra people, on-screen text, camera movement.
```
Narration: **"So I went back to work."**

## Shot 9 · 6 s · Naturalist montage · refs: Ramu Prasad (locations optional)

```
Three fast cuts, portrait frame, each a centred close-up of Ramu Prasad's hands at work with the same faded red bandana visible at the top of frame: tossing a wok over open flame; shaking a pasta pan; pulling naan from a tandoor. The hands age slightly between cuts. Warm practical light, deep shadow. Rhythmic, quick. Avoid: other people's faces, on-screen text.
```

## Shot 10 · 7 s · Naturalist · refs: Ramu Prasad, The Grand Hotel Kitchen, Delhi

```
Handheld, portrait frame. The Grand Hotel Kitchen, Delhi at full dinner service. Ramu Prasad aged thirty-five, grey at the temples, clean head-chef whites, same faded red bandana, stands at the pass calling tickets, calm, while blurred cooks and plates move fast past him. Focus stays on him; everything else motion-blurred. Warm pendant light. A controlled storm. Avoid: other cooks' faces in focus, on-screen text, named extra characters.
```
Narration: **"Then the capital."**

## Shot 11 · 7 s · Symmetry · refs: Ramu Prasad, The Grand Hotel Kitchen, Delhi

```
Flat-on, dead-centre symmetrical wide shot, portrait frame, locked-off static camera. The Grand Hotel Kitchen, Delhi after service: silent, immaculate, the central aisle receding to the vanishing point. Ramu Prasad aged thirty-five alone at the pass, slowly wiping the steel. Held long. Warm amber light. Stillness. Avoid: camera movement, other people, on-screen text.
```

## Shot 12 · 10 s · Symmetry · ENDING · refs: Ramu Prasad, The Grand Hotel Kitchen, Delhi, Steel Tea Kettle and Glasses

```
Overhead top-down shot, portrait frame: the polished steel pass of The Grand Hotel Kitchen, Delhi at dawn, twelve small ribbed tea glasses in a perfect row, one blackened steel tea kettle. Ramu Prasad's scarred hands enter the frame and pour the first glass, steam rising. Then cut to a flat-on, dead-centre wide shot: Ramu Prasad aged thirty-five, grey at the temples, head-chef whites, same faded red bandana, standing alone in the silent kitchen, his brigade filing in soft-focus behind him. He drinks from the glass, ties the bandana tighter, and looks down the line. Warm dawn light, symmetrical, held. Avoid: on-screen text in the image, other faces in focus, cool blue grading.
```
Narration: **"Twenty years. Same glass."**
Card (after): **"Back to work."** — then black.
