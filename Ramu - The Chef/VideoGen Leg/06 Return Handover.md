## What was cut and why

- The 12-shot / 80-s plan in 03 Script.md became 3 clips × 3 sub-shots (9 beats, 45 s): the Studio renders one 15-s clip per scene, three sub-shots each. Cut: the naan-hook close-up, the silhouette, the eyes, the over-the-shoulder kitchen entry, the tweezers plating, the hands montage, the ticket close-up. Kept every arc beat: dawn pour → stall wide → tandoor → pasta toss → step in rain → dawn walk-off → pass at service → silent kitchen → twelve glasses + bandana.
- Ages. The locked character reference is Ramu at 25; "fifteen" and "thirty-five" did not render, so the narration was made age-free.
- Story Studio (Ink visual novel) was investigated first and dropped once Video Studio proved workable; notes on it are in 01 Tool Notes.md if ever needed.
- The Landscape test project "The Last Ticket" was abandoned at zero spend.

## What we learned about the tool that the handover didn't have

- Two tools: Story Studio (Ink-scripted visual novel with autoplay) vs Video Studio (Seedance video). The track accepts either.
- Video Studio flow: Idea → Show Overview → Style → Summary → Characters → Locations → Props → Style Check → Storyboards → Video Shots → soundtrack/episode → publish. Orientation is locked at project creation.
- Costs: text drafting ~100; images 120–200 (Nano Banana 2 / Pro; GPT Image 2 also offered); storyboard sheets ~1,000 on Pro; **video 6,827 per 15-s shot on Seedance 2.0 Fast**, ~6–7 min each, sequential; each shot uses the previous shot's last frame. Other video models offered (Seedance 2.5, Wan 2.1/3.0, Minimax H3) — untested.
- Every image is a locked reference; a name in a prompt is not an attached reference. Character portraits come as turnaround sheets.
- Voices: 734-voice ElevenLabs library + Design/Upload; "Add speaker → Shot N+" binds a voice to a sub-shot inside a clip.
- Auto-drafted motion prompts take only panels 1–3 of a six-panel sheet and default to a teal blue-hour grade — always rewrite them.
- The balance display is rounded (k); the Video Shots banner shows the exact figure.
- Balance jumped 144k → 193.8k during the evening, unexplained.
